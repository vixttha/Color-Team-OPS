## Team remit

## Distinct engineering / operational roles
1. UX researcher — user journey clarity, friction and task completion.
2. Accessibility analyst — keyboard, semantics, assistive technology and content clarity.
3. Human-error simulator — safe mistaken inputs and recovery in authorized sandbox.
4. Prompt ambiguity analyst — underspecified intent, refusal confusion and corrective questions.
