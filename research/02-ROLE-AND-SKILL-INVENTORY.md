## Imported report's alternative (not activated)
Gold CEO/CTO/COO, program/product/engineering managers = credible real-life leadership titles, but project routes them through **Head**; Black sysadmin/platform/database/network specialists = **Green**, not a renaming of Black; the submitted report has no Gray Cell, but synthetic-user work is already present and should remain. White CISO/GRC/compliance/risk/assurance mapping can be referenced as a **capability suggestion** while retaining its restricted existing role. Do not conflate an actual human internal auditor with the workflow's distinct mandatory internal-auditor phase.

## Testable specialization definition
For every competency: scope, task families, read/write capability, authorized tools, inputs, expected artifacts, methods, minimum confidence, measurable skill evaluation, false-positive/negative handling, evidence schema, abstention/stop conditions and recommended handoff. A title alone grants NO permission.
