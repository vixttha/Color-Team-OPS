## Evidence labels and limitations
The job titles and organizational assignments above are **transcribed/adapted from the user's uploaded research** and demonstrate possible role families. The fact that some titles are recognized occupations does not make their color mapping officially verified. Verified: April Wright's six-color conceptual model; NIST NICE work-role definitions; NIST/NCCDC competition colors; NIST SSDF and OWASP DevSecOps. Not verified: nine departments are best, Gold as universal executives, all occupations enumerate color teams, or any automatic model skill from job naming.
